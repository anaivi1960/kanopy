Forest baselines you can check

The forest, measured. The measurement, checkable.

Kanopy measures a reserve from open satellite data and records a fingerprint of the boundary, the inputs, the code and the result in a public registry. This page recomputes those fingerprints in your browser and compares them with the registry. It also shows which ground is already counted for a year, so two projects cannot count it twice.

4What this does not show

  • It is not a carbon credit. The registry records measurements and who counts which ground. It does not issue, price or transfer anything.
  • The carbon figure is wide. It is above-ground biomass from a global map, and the uncertainty shown is the map's own. Nobody has walked either reserve for us.
  • A match proves the record was not changed. It does not prove the satellite products are right about the forest. That takes field plots.
  • The claims here are demonstrations. They were made with Kam Edge test keys, not by the authorities that manage these reserves, and the overlap area is a rectangle we drew.
